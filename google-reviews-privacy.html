Google review connection privacy

This notice covers the Google review connection used by Team Weekend Trekkers to display reviews of our business on our website.

Information used

When a business administrator connects Google, the integration retrieves our business’s review text, star ratings, reviewer display names and public profile photos, review dates, and overall rating and review count. Selected five-star reviews may appear on the website with a link to Google Maps. The overall rating represents all Google reviews, rather than only the displayed selection.

Google authorization

Google requests Business Profile management permission for this connection. Our integration uses it to send read-only requests for review information. Business administrators authorize the connection through Google; website visitors can read the displayed reviews without signing in.

Storage and service providers

Connection credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in our Supabase project and are used by the server. Automatic review content is cached temporarily in server memory for up to 15 minutes of use. The automatic feed is not committed to the website’s source repository or stored in visitors’ local storage.

Your controls

Business administrators can switch between automatic reviews and manual selection in the manager app. To revoke the Google connection, remove the app’s access in your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Contact us to request removal of stored connection credentials or ask about this integration.
